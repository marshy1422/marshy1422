GAME MECHANICS:

Pet Evolution System:
Stage 1: Viking Egg 🥚 (Level 1)
Stage 2: Baby Viking 👶 (Level 2-3)
Stage 3: Young Warrior ⚔️ (Level 4-6)
Stage 4: Skilled Fighter 🛡️ (Level 7-10)
Stage 5: Battle Viking ⚡ (Level 11-15)
Stage 6: Legendary Viking 👑 (Level 16+)

XP System:
- AI Chat: +10 XP per message
- Mood Tracking: +15 XP per entry
- Journal: +20 XP per entry
- CBT Exercises: +25 XP per completed exercise
- Level up requires 200 XP
- XP resets to 0 after level up

Pet Stats:
- Health: 0-100 (decreases 5 points per day)
- Happiness: 0-100 (decreases 3 points per day)
- Feed action: +10 health, +5 happiness
- Play action: +10 happiness
- Low stats affect pet appearance (looks sad/tired)
